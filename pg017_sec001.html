Chapter Two

Singing

Singing has various benefits. Singing is one of the ways used by for self-expression. It is also a way in which people express their emotions, ideas, feelings, enjoyment and satisfaction. Singing is also an income generating activity whereby singers earn incomes by selling their musical works. In this chapter, you will learn how to sing in unison using proper posture and breathing. You will also learn the importance of singing. The competencies developed will enable you to sing different songs correctly.
