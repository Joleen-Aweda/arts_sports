Activity 1

Use clay soil to do the following:

(a) Model various figures of your choice.

(b) Store those figures for art exhibitions at your school.

Benefits of modelling

Modelling has many benefits in society, including to

  • (a) promote creativity
  • (b) generate an income
  • (c) produce vessels and decorations
  • (d) keep memories
  • (e) show the culture of the community
  • (f) send a message to the community
