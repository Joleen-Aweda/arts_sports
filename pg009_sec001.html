Things to consider in acting

The following are the things to consider in acting:

(a) Presence of characters;

(b) Presence of an audience;

(c) Presence of a stage;

(d) Producing voice that can be heard well;

(e) Showing gestures and body movements;

(f) Using props and costumes properly;

(g) Using make ups properly;

(h) Managing the stage well; and

(i) Sending the message correctly.
