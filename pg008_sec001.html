Arts and Sports

Acting takes place before an audience to convey the intended message. The performance that takes place during acting is called play. Some types of play use words and actions; others do not use words; and yet others involve dance drama. A play can end happily or sadly. An actor employs body movements, facial expressions, voice, the environment and imagination. This is meant to show the reality of what is being acted. Acting involves four essentials, as follows:

  • (a) A performer, who is called an actor;
  • (b) A message, which is called a theme;
  • (c) A performing place, known as the stage; and
  • (d) People who watch the performance, known as the audience.
