Proper breathing during singing

Proper breathing helps a singer to sing without cutting off words. Thus, the singer can deliver the message correctly. To breathe properly, the singer should do breathing exercises. An example is breathing in and out slowly. This exercise enables the singer to inhale during singing. When the singer inhales properly, he or she can sing without cutting off words. For example, in a song like Mungu Ibariki Afrika, the singer should not pause before finishing all the words in the first sentence.
