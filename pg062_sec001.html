Running with a bottle balanced on the head

In this traditional game, players compete by running with bottles balanced on their heads. This game is fun and competitive. It also helps to strengthen body balance.

How to play

  1. 1. Exercise to prepare your body.
  2. 2. Mark the starting and ending points of the play area.
  3. 3. Stand at the starting point.
  4. 4. Balance the bottle on your head.
Figure 3: Pupils waiting for the command of the leader
  1. 5. Wait for the command of the leader.
  2. 6. After the command, start running without holding the bottle.
  3. 7. Run faster than others without dropping the bottle.
