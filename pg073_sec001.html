Things to consider when participating in singing shows

When participating in singing shows, consider the following points.

1.

Select a song for the show. It is good to choose a song that you like. It should also have an attractive message. The song should touch the lives of people in the community;

2.

Have enough time to practise. The practise should involve vocal exercises and singing. If you are to sing the song as a group, practise singing it with your fellows. Good preparation makes the singer confident.
