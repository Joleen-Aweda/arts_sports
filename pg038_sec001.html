Activity 1

Use a pencil and paper to draw a picture of an item found in your classroom.

Steps to be followed in drawing a picture using a pencil

To produce a meaningful picture, consider the following steps.

  1. 1. Choose the objects you want to draw;
  2. 2. Prepare paper and pencils for drawing;
  3. 3. Start drawing the picture you want using light lines;
  4. 4. Use the rubber to correct parts you have drawn incorrectly; and
  5. 5. Bold the picture lines based on the message accuracy and appearance.

Remember to draw freely without using measurements. Appearance is what matters.
