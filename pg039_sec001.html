Activity 2

Use a pencil and paper to draw shapes of fruits, furniture and utensils. Draw freely without using measurements. Their appearances are what matter.

Importance of drawing pictures

Pictures are drawn for the following benefits.

  • (a)Selling them and getting money
  • (b)Decorating different places
  • (c)Keeping records of events
  • (d)Educating and entertaining society
  • (e)Developing long-term memories
  • (f)Illustrating and simplifying an understanding of concepts
Activity 3

Using a pencil and paper, draw a picture showing pupils playing at school. Draw freely without using measurements. Use only a pencil and rubber. Do not use a ruler or any other tool to help you to draw. Its appearance is more important.
