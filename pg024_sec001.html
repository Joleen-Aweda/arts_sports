Musical instruments
Introduction

Music is made up of different sounds. These sounds can be of people or musical instruments. The voices of singers, when accompanied by sounds of musical instruments, make the music nice and interesting. They also add various feelings. In this chapter, you will learn how to use musical instruments to accompany short songs. You will also learn how to make and play musical instruments. The competencies developed will enable you to make and play musical instruments found in your environment. Therefore, you can later generate an income.
