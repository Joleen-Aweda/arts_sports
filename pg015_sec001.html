Importance of acting

Acting is very important in society, as follows:

(a)Entertaining;
(b)Educating;
(c)Generating an income;
(d)Preserving culture;
(e)Improving health;
(f)Conveying a message;
(g)Developing speaking skills;
(h)Developing confidence;
(i)Identifying and developing talents; and
(j)Developing creativity.
