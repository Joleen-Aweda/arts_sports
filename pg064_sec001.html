Running with an egg on a spoon

This is a traditional game in which players compete to run with an egg or a lemon on a spoon. The spoon can be held in the hand or mouth. This game is fun and competitive. This game also strengthens the body’s balance and agility.

Tools: Spoon, lemon or egg

How to play

  1. 1. Exercise to prepare your body.
  2. 2. Mark the starting and ending points of the play area.
  3. 3. Stand at the starting point.
  4. 4. Hold the spoon in your hand and put the lemon or egg on it.
