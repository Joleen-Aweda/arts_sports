Benefits of playing rede

This rede game has several benefits. It helps to make players

  • 1. develop active bodies;
  • 2. build friendship and teamwork;
  • 3. develop numeracy skills;
  • 4. improve the ability to think;
  • 5. strengthen their bodies through physical exercise;
  • 6. develop the competitive spirit; and
  • 7. develop speaking skills.
