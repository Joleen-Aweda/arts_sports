Chapter Four

Drawing

Introduction

Drawing is a part of visual arts. This art uses pictures to convey messages to society. In this chapter, you will learn the meaning of pictures. You will also learn the importance of drawing pictures expressing different emotions using a pencil. The competencies developed will enable you to draw different pictures.

Meaning of a picture

A picture is a representation of a real object. It can be designed, drawn or taken with a camera. You can see pictures in your environment, at school, in books, in magazines, on posters and on clothes.
